description: A licensed bank or network member that gives a non-member company access to card networks under its membership.
---

A sponsor bank is a licensed bank, and member of a [card network](./Card%20network.md), that lets a company without its own membership take part in card payments. A fintech issuing cards, a non-bank [payment processor](./Payment%20processor.md) or a [payment facilitator](./Payment%20facilitator.md) can operate under the sponsor's membership, while the sponsor remains answerable to the network and its regulators.

Sponsorship can sit on either side of a transaction.

| Side      | Sponsored company                         | What the sponsor provides                                     |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Issuing   | Fintech card program or program manager   | The [issuer](./Issuer.md) role, [BIN](./BIN.md) access, regulated accounts |
| Acquiring | Independent sales organisation, PayFac    | [Acquirer](./Acquirer.md) membership and access to [settlement](./Settlement.md)   |

A sponsor bank differs from a partner bank that only holds deposits. The sponsor's network membership is what gives the company access to the card rails. It also differs from a processor, which runs technology but does not itself provide the membership.

The model is common in the US, where non-bank companies frequently reach Visa and Mastercard through a sponsoring bank. It is not universal. The Bank of England's guide for FCA-authorised non-bank providers says it is possible to be a direct participant in Visa and Mastercard in most respects while settling through another organisation. In the EU, [PSD2](./PSD2.md) generally requires payment system access rules to be objective, non-discriminatory and proportionate. Each network sets its own eligibility criteria.

Because the sponsor carries the regulatory exposure, it sets conditions on the program. It may review marketing, require [KYC](./KYC.md) and [AML](./AML.md) controls, and audit the company's operations. If a sponsor exits a program or a regulator restricts it, the company can lose card access or need to migrate customers to a new BIN, which means new card numbers for customers. Check what the contract says about termination and transition.

_Usage:_

"Why does compliance need to approve the copy for our card launch?"

"We issue under our sponsor bank's membership. If our marketing breaks its rules, it's their licence at risk."
